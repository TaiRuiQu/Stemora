Stemora bundles 30 anchor notes (A0–C8). Changes: decoded from MP3 to
44.1 kHz mono PCM16, trimmed onset silence (keeping 1 ms pre-roll), capped
at 10 seconds, faded the final 50 ms and gzip-compressed. Playback interpolates
between anchor pitches, adjusts sample gain, and applies short attack/release
ramps. The source revision, source hashes, PCM hashes and exact trimming are
recorded in src/Stemora.Core/Data/Piano/manifest.json.
